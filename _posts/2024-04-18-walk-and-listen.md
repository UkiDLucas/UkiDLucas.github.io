> The best sailing is down the trade winds.
> If you are setting off to do something, go with the prevailing winds.

Every couple of years, I reevaluate which way the wind is blowing and make corrections in my life.

The last couple of years have been dominated by AI and autonomy.
Some jobs are disappearing, and some are on the rise.

You must always trim the sails in response to the wind and the waves.
You must also set the right pace for how far you still have to go.

Today, I want to share my two favorite activities: walking and listening.

During the week, I cannot go to the mountains, so I walk on the path by my house.
The path is about 12,000 steps, which makes my Apple device feel very satisfied.

On a beautiful evening like today, there are a few stumps and logs where I sit down, soak up the sun, and think.
Along the way, I stop to smell the roses, or rather, to take photos of the flowers and the bunnies.

The second, and quite important, part of this hobby is listening.
I usually listen to an Audible audiobook, or to a YouTube video when I want to learn something.

The walk charges me full of ideas.
Some are actions to take, and some are things to write about.

## Where am I going?

I have limited time before I retire or otherwise stop working.
I have to prepare now, so that I can retire and do what I love.

The best way to achieve it is to secure a residual income, care for my health, and have a purpose.
I want to walk, listen, think, and write.
I hope to have readers.

## Where is the rest of the world going?

AI and automation will change the landscape of people's lives.

How-to blogs will lose relevance, because AI chatbots will answer any question you might have.
Wise fatherly, or grandfatherly, advice may lose its relevance for the same reason.

My only hope for writing is to write from the heart, to be a human, an anthropologist, a philosopher, and a poet.

The future of humanity will be about human entertainment, the service industry, and human contact, in a world filled with AI games, chatbots, robots, and automated factories.

## How do I connect with my readers?

I need to engage with my audience.
So far, the blog has failed at this: not a single comment in a very long time, not a single direct message.

I will explore Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn (with 13,000 contacts), Threads, and Pinterest, but I no longer enjoy these interactions.
I used to wait for the pings and notifications.
Now I turn them off.

Still, I want to hear from you.
What did you think of my articles?
What would you like me to write about?
Which of your problems or questions can I address?

Respectfully,
Uki D. Lucas
