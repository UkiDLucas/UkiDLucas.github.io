Taking as a fact that you have a great app, a "shouting", cutting-edge presence on the app markets, a good rating and good promotion, what else could influence the number of your app downloads?

## Updates

Having over a dozen apps out on the market, we noticed that every time we release an update, we have a spike in downloads.

The reason is that when you publish an update, the app market gives you a boost in ranking. You show up more often in the search results and may be listed on the first page of ratings within the category your app belongs to.

In addition, surprising users with improvements (hopefully), either some new features or a holiday re-skinning of the UI, works as a gentle reminder. It can increase the number of active users, which is very important.

## Weekends

Another observation, more obvious this time, is that people tend to download and explore new apps on weekends.

Here is an example of the download statistics on [ChildrenTV](https://market.android.com/details?id=com.chicagoandroid.childrentv). On the right side you see the number of downloads; on the left I showed the weekend days.

## Know your users

Another piece of advice would be: know your users.

Try to analyze and put yourself in your user's shoes. Imagine his life cycle and try to understand at what point you (your app) can be useful. When is your user more willing to use your product?

Here is an example from the same app, Children TV. Take a guess what is the reason for the massive increase in downloads in these statistics.

![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjj-3pmFgqAX9TgDsH-2B8Lc5VRjwYtdZSn8gTh5g8A-Py_3DtZdg8ZDdo6hYH52rFYFfLA-XUiR9g7Ov8o2ZZqJLHp4ivaLOa0wnfJpxl4H5C5H7McseWTHRqPjcWIvQhonGoBPw/s1600/cyber2.png)

If you pay attention to the dates, you will see that on vacation the downloads are considerably higher. Parents are happy to get their kids busy, and kids are happy to watch their favorite cartoons. Boom, mission accomplished.
